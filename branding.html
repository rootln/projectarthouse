01 What it isBranding

We start with what makes you different and work outward: the name, the logo, the colours, the type, and how they behave on a box, a bill and a phone screen.

You leave with files you can use the same day and a short guide that keeps the brand consistent when other people work on it.

03 QuestionsBranding

Asked often.

What does a branding project include?

A branding project usually covers the name if needed, the logo and its variations, colours, typefaces and a short guideline, delivered as ready-to-use files. Packaging and print are added when you need them.

Can you refresh a brand that already exists?

Yes. We look at what you have, keep what works and redraw the rest, so customers still recognise you.

How is branding priced?

By scope. We talk first, then send a written quote before any work begins.
