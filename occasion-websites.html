01 What it isOccasion websites

This is where the studio began. We take your photos, stories and messages and turn them into a small website that belongs to one occasion: a birthday, a wedding, a Grihapravesh, Father's Day, Valentine's Day.

There is nothing to install. The person you send it to taps a link and the page opens, with their name on it.

What occasion websites covers

  1. 01

    Birthday reveals

    One-day, three-day and seven-day experiences with photos, letters and a final reveal.

  2. 02

    Wedding invitations

    Couple names, your story, the programme and a private link to share.

  3. 03

    Grihapravesh and family invitations

    Date, venue, timing and a family message for a housewarming, puja or gathering.

  4. 04

    Father's Day and Valentine's

    Tributes and love notes, from a single page to an eight-day Valentine's Week.

03 QuestionsOccasion websites

Asked often.

What goes on an occasion website?

Your photos, your messages and the story in the order you want it told. An invitation also carries the date, venue and programme. All of it sits behind one private link that opens on any phone.

How is an occasion website delivered?

You receive a private link. Send it by WhatsApp, text or email and it opens in the browser. Nobody has to install an app or create an account.

Can I see one before I order?

Yes. Open the sample birthday page made for a fictional Aarya, or the sample wedding invitation. Both are linked from the plans.
