    07 QuestionsShort answers

    Asked often.

    What is Project Arthouse?

    Project Arthouse is a marketing studio from India. It runs social media and ads, builds websites and landing pages, and sets up CRM and follow-up automation, as one team working from one plan. It also makes invitations and occasion websites.

    Do I have to take every service?

    No. Take one service or all four. They come from the same studio, so anything you add later fits with what is already there.

    Which ad platforms and CRMs do you work with?

    For ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google Search and YouTube. For CRM, usually Zoho or HubSpot, or the tool you already pay for.

    How much does it cost?

    Marketing work is quoted after a conversation, because scope varies. You get scope, timeline and cost in writing before any work begins. Invitations have fixed prices, from ₹1,199 or $39.99.

    Do you still make invitations?

    Yes. Birthday reveals, wedding invitations, Grihapravesh invitations, Father's Day and Valentine's pages, with fixed prices on the rate card.

    Do you work with clients outside India?

    Yes. The studio is based in India and works online with clients anywhere.

    How do I start?

    Send a brief through the form, by WhatsApp or by email. A few lines is enough. We reply in writing before any work begins.
