    06 QuestionsShort answers

    Asked often.

    What is Project Arthouse?

    Project Arthouse is a marketing studio from India. It makes occasion websites for birthdays, weddings and celebrations, brand identities and business websites, and manages social media pages.

    What is an occasion website?

    An occasion website is a small personal website made for one event or one person, such as a birthday reveal, a wedding invitation or a Grihapravesh invitation. It is shared as a private link and opens on any phone.

    How much does an occasion website cost?

    Occasion websites have fixed prices, from ₹799 to ₹1,699, or $39.99 to $79.99 in US dollars: 1 Day Birthday ₹799 ($39.99), 3 Day Curated Reveal ₹999 ($59.99), 7 Day Unfolding Experience ₹1,299 ($69.99), Wedding Invitation ₹899 ($49.99), Grihapravesh ₹899 ($49.99), Father's Day ₹899 ($49.99), Valentine's Day (1 Day) ₹799 ($39.99) and Valentine's Week (8 Days) ₹1,699 ($79.99).

    How much do websites, branding and social media cost?

    These are quoted after a conversation, because scope varies. Share your idea through the form, by WhatsApp or by email and we reply with scope, timeline and cost in writing.

    Can I hire Project Arthouse for only one service?

    Yes. You can take one service or all four. Each comes from the same studio, so they fit together if you add more later.

    Do you work with clients outside India?

    Yes. The studio is based in India and works online with clients anywhere.

    How do I start a project?

    Pick a plan on the rate card, or send a brief through the form, by WhatsApp or by email. We reply in writing before any work begins.
